Help

I bought Gnomad Plus on another iPhone

Open Levels, tap any locked level, then tap Restore. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Where is my progress kept?

On your iPhone. Your best runs, rare stamps and hats stay there between launches. Deleting the app deletes them, and Levels → Reset clears them on purpose.

A level feels impossible

Every level has been cleared by touch, start to finish. Mailboxes are checkpoints, and you can turn them off in the pause menu once you want the gold postmark. If you're stuck, write to me and say which level and where.

The music is too loud, or I can't hear it

Each level has its own song. Pause and switch Music off or on. Gnomad plays sound even with the ring switch on silent, so use the volume buttons.

What does "Anonymous stats" do?

It sends anonymous counts that help me make the levels better, like where runs end. Switch it off in the pause menu at any time. The privacy policy explains exactly what is sent.
