Gnomad

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Gnomad is a game made by Ivo Iturrieta. It has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking across apps. The only thing it sends is anonymous gameplay stats, and you can switch those off.

What stays on your iPhone. Your progress (best runs, attempts, rare stamps, hats and settings) is saved on your iPhone. It never leaves it and is deleted when you delete the app.

What Gnomad sends. To learn which levels are fun and where they're too hard, Gnomad sends anonymous stats to PostHog, an analytics service. For example: that a level was started or cleared, how many attempts it took, where a run ended, which settings were changed, the frame rate, memory warnings, whether the sound is playing and through what kind of output (the speaker or headphones, never a device's name), and the screens visited. Each event carries a random app ID that isn't linked to you, the app version, and how much memory the device has (rounded). Gnomad doesn't store your IP address's location, doesn't record your screen and doesn't collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

Turning the stats off. Pause any level and switch Anonymous stats off. From then on Gnomad sends nothing, and it remembers your choice.

Purchases. Gnomad Plus is bought through Apple, and Gnomad never sees your payment details. The stats note that Plus was offered, bought or restored, never who you are.

Children. Gnomad doesn't ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
